# NDAP Database Catalogs

This directory contains schemas, relational DDL specifications, and sample Parquet exports from the **20 National Data & Analytics Platform (NDAP)** databases evaluated in the IndicDB benchmark (April 2026).

## Key Domains Covered:
- **Agriculture**: PM-KISAN, Fertilizer sales, mandi crop prices, and agriculture census.
- **Education**: UDISE+ school enrollment, teacher count, infrastructure indices, and dropout statistics.
- **Healthcare & Demographics**: NFHS-5 surveys, rural health clinics, and maternal mortality.
- **Rural Development & Employment**: MGNREGA person-days generated, job cards issued, and wage disbursements.
